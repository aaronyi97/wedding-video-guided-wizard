# Wedding story card

[中文](story-intake.md) | **English**

Fill in this template and send it to your creator in your usual messaging app, or open `story-intake.html` and select English for one-click copying. Desktop is recommended; mobile works too. Photos are sent separately. No phone number, account or backend submission is required.

Use only real experiences. For any question, “Other: no such event / don't remember / don't include this” is a valid answer; say the same in the detail field. Examples are prompts, not facts to copy. Except for optional extras and music, answer each item; write “None” in notes if applicable.

## The basics

1. Aspect ratio: [x] Landscape 16:9 (default, venue screen) [ ] Portrait 9:16 (phone screen) [ ] Other: ___
2. Names to use in the film: Groom ___ / Bride ___
3. Send clear front-facing photos together or individually in your conversation with the creator. Early relationship, wedding outfit or makeup photos can help. No photo upload here. A photo taken by someone else can avoid close-up selfie distortion.
4. When will it play? [ ] Before the ceremony [ ] During the ceremony [ ] Reception opening [ ] Other: ___
5. Preferred duration: [x] 90 seconds [ ] 180 seconds [ ] Other: ___
   Actual shot timing will follow the completed narration, not a fixed number of seconds per shot.
6. Visual style: [ ] Cinematic realism [ ] Watercolor storybook [ ] Paper craft/origami [ ] Clay stop-motion [ ] Playful 3D animation [ ] Other: ___
   For a stylized or other look: [ ] Real people + stylized setting [ ] Fully animated people [ ] Photorealistic people [ ] Other: ___

## Music · Optional

22. Instrumental track: [ ] I have one in mind—title/artist/link: ___ [ ] Let the creator choose or generate music
    Instrumental music leaves space for narration. A meaningful song mentioned as a story detail need not become the soundtrack.

## Notes · Required

23. Look and feel, film/photo references, important details, pronunciation or anything to watch for: ___

Narration uses third-person storytelling. Return the completed text and original photos separately. The creator will organize the facts for your check before writing; missing or uncertain facts must not become invented events.
