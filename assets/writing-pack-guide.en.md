# Building the copyable writing prompt

[中文](writing-pack-guide.md) | **English**

Use the approved film language from the brief and `content_language`; English instructions do not override a Chinese film. Apply English prose and word budgeting below only to English films; for Chinese films use natural Chinese narration and its character-based planning. Never translate an approved script merely to change the interface.

The assistant fills every section for the current couple. This guide is not the deliverable: the creator receives an actual completed WRITING_PROMPT.txt and ZIP with the entire prompt embedded.

1. **Task:** film purpose, playback occasion, requested runtime, narration language and point of view.
2. **Style:** understated but vivid, natural spoken English, warmth through real details, varied sentence rhythm and supported structural echoes. Explain what to avoid: ornate abstractions, slogans and a dry itinerary. Do not copy a past couple's events.
3. **Confirmed facts:** all usable intake facts, source IDs, chronology, names and pronunciations, plus explicit omissions and uncertainties. No invented important props, quotes or experiences.
4. **Narrative plan:** the supported events to emphasize, paragraph order/weight and feasible visual ideas. This is not the formal storyboard; actual full narration comes first.
5. **Constraints:** estimate an English word budget from runtime without copying a Chinese character quota; preserve pauses and meaning. Do not infer unreported customs or relationships. No “see attachment,” missing context or unfilled placeholders.
6. **Deliverable:** one complete read-aloud narration. Keep comments and any factual questions separate from the pure narration; never turn uncertain details into fictional lines.

Strongly recommend Kimi K3. Give clear instructions to return the whole draft, review it personally, then forward the exact approved version to the couple before any visual production.
